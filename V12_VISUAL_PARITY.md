# Wingman v12 — Replit Mockup Visual Parity Pass

This build keeps the v11.2 functionality/checklist and restores the approved mobile visual language from the earlier Replit mockup.

## Visual corrections
- Restored the approved suited Wingman logo asset in the app header and center navigation.
- Reworked the hero to use the red/black broadcast treatment with the Wingman character on the right.
- Flattened oversized/glossy cards into tighter broadcast cards with a subtle bevel and restrained white rim glow.
- Rebuilt the bottom navigation as a flatter black bar with the raised circular Wingman button.
- Reduced corner radii and excessive whitespace to better match the original mobile mockup.
- Changed the home quick tools to compact controls instead of tall tiles.
- Made The Action game cards more compact, with team/score and Spread / Moneyline / Total aligned in one table-style card.
- Kept Bets % / Money % and Watch / Hot / Very Hot indicators directly inside the corresponding market cells when legitimate split data exists.
- Reworked the dedicated Wingman page to mirror the original Ask Wingman result screen rather than a generic dashboard card.

## Functional acceptance criteria preserved
- The Action / The Numbers / Wingman / The Word / The Huddle
- Profile/Settings top-right
- MLB/NFL/NBA/NCAAF/NCAAB
- Complete/dynamic team directories and searchable selectors
- Multi-sport Ask Your Wingman architecture
- Game Center and box scores
- Historical betting fallback chain and honest no-data handling
- Player props provider-gated; no fabricated history
- The Word and The Huddle separation
- Future provider-gated sportsbook/line movement features remain honest
